How to play: type on your keyboard (or tap the keys above), just like in the Windows console. Pick a player with 1 or 2, deal with d, fold your lowest-ranked card with c, then see the score with r.
Note: this is the original main.cpp compiled to WebAssembly, including its quirks. For example, the player menu reads the keyboard twice, so you need to press 2 twice to pick Player 2.
